⚖️ Risk Scoring Explanation

The overall risk rating ($0.0 - 10.0$) is calculated deterministically based on financial exposure and statutory non-compliance:

Overall Score = min(10.0, ∑ Category_Weight * Severity)
  • High Potential Risk (8.0 - 10.0): Uncapped financial indemnity or regulatory violations.
  • Medium Risk (5.0 - 7.9): Unfavorable dispute venues or tight payment penalties.
  • Low Risk (0.0 - 4.9): Standard commercial boilerplate terms.
